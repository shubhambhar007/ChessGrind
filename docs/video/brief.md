# ChessGrind product film — working brief

Status: checkpoint build in progress  
Product source: `https://chessgrind.fyi` captured 2026-11-01  
Source revision: `ca2b8f9`

## Objective

Make a short launch film that explains ChessGrind's real differentiator in one pass: a missed chess position does not disappear after the game. ChessGrind remembers it, schedules it, and brings it back until the pattern sticks.

The film should earn curiosity and a click, not enumerate every feature.

## Locked inputs and assumptions

- Deliverables: 16:9 horizontal and 9:16 vertical.
- Target length: about 30 seconds.
- Audience: chess players frustrated by disposable puzzles, external-account requirements, and training that does not remember their mistakes.
- Voice: no voice-over. Copy, product motion, and music carry the story.
- Brand: dark navy editorial world; ivory type; electric blue interaction states; restrained brass used only for the mark and completion accents.
- Product evidence: real ChessGrind UI and real ChessGrind piece assets. No fake browser chrome and no generic dashboard mockup.
- CTA: `Train free at chessgrind.fyi`.
- Pricing is deliberately absent from this film.
- Founder, Opening Lab, and Insights are supporting proof—not separate feature chapters.

## One visual argument

The same blue legal-move marker travels from the chessboard into Grindbook as a memory card, then returns as a scheduled review. The audience should see a memory being formed, not a feature-list montage.

## Motion and visual direction

- Restrained, decisive dark UI inspired by Linear's product-film grammar.
- Hard cuts only on major beats; most elements ease in and then hold confidently.
- One signature shared-element handoff: blue move marker → saved-position memory card.
- Camera movement belongs to the whole stage. Cards do not bob or breathe independently.
- The logo appears clearly at the close; the emblem is not used as background decoration.

## Script and scene plan

| Time | Picture | On-screen copy | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4.4s | Near-black field. A blue move marker resolves beside the line, then opens the chess world. | **Your mistakes should remember you.** | Hook and thesis. |
| 4.4–10.2s | Real blue/ivory board. A premium knight is selected; every legal square appears in blue. One position is marked as worth keeping. | **Play. Notice the miss.** | First product proof. |
| 10.2–16.1s | The selected square/card transfers into a populated Grindbook queue. | **Save it once.** | Show capture, not a separate app. |
| 16.1–21.8s | Calendar/review rhythm advances; the same position returns. | **It comes back before you forget.** | Explain spaced review. |
| 21.8–26.6s | Compact proof rail: 1,000 puzzles, 200 opening drills, private memory, progress insight. | **One training loop. Built around you.** | Breadth without montage sprawl. |
| 26.6–30s | ChessGrind emblem and wordmark settle on dark navy. | **Play. Notice. Remember.**  
**Train free at chessgrind.fyi** | Brand and CTA. |

The checkpoint contains the first two rows only. The remaining rows are written but will not be animated until the checkpoint is approved.
